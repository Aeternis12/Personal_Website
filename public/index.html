About Me

I first came into Florida State University as a history major. My love of the past, and why the numerous things today are the way they are drove me to study history for the beginning two years of college. However, I came to realize that my love of history aligned better with being a hobby than a career.

My second choice was always Computer Science. I love puzzling through bugs, and seeing a project come together from nothing. I am particularly interested in Web Development, and enjoy putting together personal projects that feel actually useful. I am also interested in game development, having worked with a group to make a cloned version of the famous Bloon Tower Defense game.

Finally I do like Cybersecurity as a topic. In a world that is sacrificing more and more privacy and security for convenience, being somehow who knows how to configure and protect the projects I create is an Immensely useful tool.
